About Me

I am currently an undergraduate student at the National University of Defense Technology (NUDT), majoring in Information Engineering in the Qian Class. I graduated from the High School Affiliated to Nanjing Normal University. My academic interests lie in machine learning, remote sensing image processing, and multimodal AIGC. Beyond research, I enjoy film studies, swimming, and exploring classical & contemporary cinema aesthetics.

Research Interests

  • Image Generation: diffusion- and transformer-based generative models for controllable, high‑fidelity synthesis and cross‑domain translation.
  • Multimodal Learning: aligning text–image–sensor modalities (e.g., optical ↔ radar, text ↔ image) for translation, retrieval, and semantic grounding.
